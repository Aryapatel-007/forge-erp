Forge ERP

This installs Forge ERP on this Mac and runs it in the background, starting with the Mac.

Setup downloads Node.js (about 50 MB) and MongoDB (about 70 MB) for this Mac's chip, and checks each against the checksum its maker publishes before using it. Keep this Mac connected to the internet until setup finishes.

Your data, settings and daily backups are kept in /Library/Application Support/Forge ERP/data. If Forge ERP is already installed, setup updates it and keeps your data.

Needs macOS 11 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.
