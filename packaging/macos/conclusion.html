Forge ERP is installed

Open Forge ERP from Applications, or go to http://localhost:4100/ in your browser.

On a first install, your sign-in is in Forge ERP sign-in.txt on your Desktop. Change the password after signing in, then delete that file.

Copy the backups in /Library/Application Support/Forge ERP/data/backups to another disk. A backup on the same disk does not survive losing it.

To remove Forge ERP, run this in Terminal:
sudo "/Library/Application Support/Forge ERP/program/setup/uninstall.sh"

If something went wrong, the details are in /Library/Logs/Forge ERP/install.log.
